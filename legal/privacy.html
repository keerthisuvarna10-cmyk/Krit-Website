Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information KRIT collects when you visit www.kritsleep.in, create an account or buy from us, how we use it, and the choices you have. KRIT is a brand of Kritniv Enterprises, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("KRIT", "we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, pincode and the products you buy.
  • Account details: if you create an account, your name, email and/or mobile number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. If you sign in with a mobile OTP, we receive your verified mobile number.
  • Payment information: online payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive the payment status and reference number. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device and browser type, collected through Google Analytics only if you accept cookies.

How we use your information

  • To process, pack, ship and deliver your order, and to handle returns and refunds.
  • To send order confirmations, payment updates and delivery notifications by SMS, WhatsApp and email.
  • To let you sign in, see your orders and fill in checkout faster.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To keep accounting and tax records as required by Indian law.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it (only with your cookie consent).

Google sign-in data. When you sign in with Google, we use your name, email address and profile picture only to create and identify your KRIT account, show your name in your account, fill in your checkout details and send you order updates. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that run our website and deliver your order.

We never sell your personal information and we never share it with advertisers.

Cookies

We use essential cookies and browser storage to keep your cart, wishlist and sign-in working. Google Analytics cookies are used only if you choose Accept cookies. You can decline, and the shop still works fully. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is active. We keep order and invoice records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, then delete them. If you delete your account, we remove your account details but keep order records we are legally required to retain.

How we protect it

Your information is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and stored with access limited to the people who need it to run KRIT.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it, or to delete your account and personal information (except records we must keep by law).
  • Stop Google sign-in access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics cookies by clearing cookies in your browser.

Email hello@kritsleep.in from the email or phone linked to your order or account. We reply within 7 business days.

Children

The website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
